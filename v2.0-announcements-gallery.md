# v2.0 — Announcements + Gallery

Milestone release.

## Announcements
- Founder announcement creation
- Draft/publish behavior
- Audiences: members, organizers, everyone, public
- In-app announcement notifications
- Member announcement feed
- Public announcement feed

## Gallery
- Founder album creation
- Member-only default visibility
- Photo URL + caption management
- Member gallery and album pages
- Public visibility can be added deliberately rather than being the default

## Cost architecture
Photos are represented by URLs in v2.0. No paid storage provider is required by the app itself.
